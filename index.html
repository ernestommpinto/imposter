How to Play

1. Choose 4 to 10 players.

2. Pick a category and start the match.

3. Each player secretly sees one word.

4. One player has a different word. That player is the impostor.

5. Discuss, accuse and vote privately.

6. First side to win the 5-round match becomes champion.
